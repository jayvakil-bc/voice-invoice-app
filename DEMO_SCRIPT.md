## 🎤 Q&A PREP - Common Questions

### Q: "How accurate is the AI?"
> "GPT-4o has 95%+ accuracy on structured data extraction. Users can always edit before saving."

### Q: "What about privacy/security?"
> "All data encrypted. Google OAuth for secure login. We're SOC 2 compliant-ready architecture."

### Q: "How do you make money?"
> "Two ways: Monthly subscription ($10-15) and optional platform fee (1%) on payments processed."

### Q: "Why not just use QuickBooks?"
> "QuickBooks is complex, expensive ($30-90/mo), and still requires manual data entry. We're voice-first, AI-powered, and 3x cheaper."

### Q: "What's your competitive advantage?"
> "Voice + AI is our moat. No one else has cracked voice-to-invoice with this level of accuracy. Plus we're building for freelancers specifically, not enterprises."

### Q: "How much does AI cost you?"
> "$0.03-0.05 per invoice. At $10/month, users need to create 200-300 invoices to cost us more than they pay. Average freelancer: 10-20/month."

### Q: "What's the tech stack?"
> "Node.js/Express backend, MongoDB database, OpenAI API, Stripe, Google APIs. Fully modern, scalable architecture."

### Q: "Can this scale?"
> "Absolutely. Stateless architecture, MongoDB for horizontal scaling, cloud-ready (AWS/GCP). We can handle millions of users."
